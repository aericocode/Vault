7-Zip 26.06  -  7-Zip/7za.exe (Windows), 7-Zip/7zzs (Linux)
------------------------------------------------------------
Packs the files for Share privately into a password-protected 7z archive.
Shipped unmodified, as published by its author: 7za.exe from "7-Zip Extra"
(7z2606-extra.7z) and 7zzs from "7-Zip for Linux" (7z2606-linux-x64.tar.xz).
In a source checkout they are in vendor/7zip/, with each download's own
License.txt.
