hls.js 1.7.2  —  player-lib/hls.min.js
--------------------------------------
Plays HLS streams in browsers that have Media Source Extensions. Vault uses it
for files that have to be remuxed on the fly (see lib/stream/).

Microsoft WebView2 SDK 1.0.3240.44  —  runtime/webview2/ (not embedded)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stage window (lib/stage/) shows its page in WebView2, the browser engine
Windows 10 and 11 ship as the WebView2 Runtime. Vault carries three files of
the SDK (NuGet package Microsoft.Web.WebView2): Microsoft.Web.WebView2.Core.dll,
Microsoft.Web.WebView2.WinForms.dll and WebView2Loader.dll (x64), in
runtime/webview2/ next to Vault.exe, with this licence as LICENSE.txt.

Homepage: https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
