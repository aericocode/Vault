Wall mode · New in 3.9

Your whole collection, playing at once.

The Wall fills the screen with a collage of your own clips and pictures. A few videos play at a time, pictures cycle, and every block keeps its file's shape. Put it on and let it run.

  • Grid holds still and swaps blocks on a timer; Scroll drifts the whole board upward, forever
  • Pause on hover holds a block, and the drift, while you look
  • Sound Off, On hover, or Auto - Auto hands the sound to the clip with the most time left
  • Click a playing clip and it carries on in the player from the same moment
  • Feed it the current filter, a collection, or a saved search

Music ID · The one nothing else has

Find every video that shares a song. Watch them in sync.

Local audio fingerprinting matches tracks across your whole collection, then stacks the matches into one synced and layered player - with a full mixer per track. It needs your library to work, so no site ever could.

  • Auto-align on the shared track; fixed waveforms, a moving playhead to fine-tune live
  • Per-layer opacity, blend modes, volume, spotlight - save the mix as a playable tile
  • "Similar audio" ranks your library by tracks in common - a sort no filename can fake

Face search · Studios

Search by who's in it.

Vault finds the people in every photo, GIF and video, then sorts them into a Faces tab you can name and play. Click a face and see every file that person is in.

  • Search with a photo, or pause a video and find who's on screen
  • Name people once; "Same person?" asks before it merges anyone
  • Two face models: a fast one for the first pass, a sharper one to tell people apart
  • Studios group files and people by where they come from
  • Off until you turn it on. One download of about 213 MB, then it works offline and uploads nothing

Subtitles

Every video gets real subtitles. Even the foreign ones.

One click transcribes the whole file on your own GPU - talking and singing - translates foreign audio to English, and colors each speaker. Then every spoken line becomes searchable from the library.

  • Off / On / Auto - Auto shows English only when the audio isn't English
  • Covers the whole file, start to finish, including the songs
  • Per-speaker colors when voices are separated; drag the captions anywhere
  • Wrong line? "Fix the lines around here" re-scans just the minute around the playhead
  • Generate on demand or during a scan - all local Whisper, zero cloud

🔐 One password seals it all

Everything Vault writes about your files - titles, tags, notes, watch history, thumbnails, previews, subtitles, faces - becomes unreadable noise without your password.

details: SQLite-level encryption (better-sqlite3-multiple-ciphers), ChaCha20-Poly1305 by default - AES-256-class. No hand-rolled crypto anywhere.
🗑 Deletes that can't bite you

The trash button moves files, it never erases them - with one-click Undo. Want more? Choose Recycle Bin or true permanent delete per session.

details: move-only engine with copy+verify across drives; trash names are opaque <id>.<ext> - originals live only in the encrypted DB. Hard delete is opt-in.
🔑 Secured Metadata

Vault encrypts everything it creates. Your original media files stay untouched wherever they already live - pair Vault with your OS disk encryption for full coverage.

details: add or change the passphrase any time (PRAGMA rekey, in place, both stores). BitLocker / FileVault / Secure Drive covers the media at rest.
