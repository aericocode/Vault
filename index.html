Wall mode · Let it run

Your whole collection, playing at once.

The Wall fills the screen with a collage of your own clips and pictures. A few videos play at a time, pictures cycle, and every block keeps its file's shape. Put it on and let it run.

  • Two layouts: Horizontal fills the screen with full-width rows, Vertical stacks columns. Pick Small, Medium or Large, and Even, Mixed or Wild sizes
  • Grid holds still and swaps blocks at the pace you pick; Scroll drifts the whole board, forever
  • Sound Off, On hover, Follow a clip, or rotate between clips every 10 to 60 seconds
  • Videos on Auto or a number you set; open a clip from the full-screen Wall and it plays full screen, Escape takes you back
  • Feed it the current filter, a collection, or a saved search

Playlists · Flow

Line it up once. Let it run.

Every collection is a playlist. Put it in order by hand, or let Flow order it so each file leads into the next.

  • Drag tiles or table rows into your own Playlist order, and mark a file Skip so playback passes over it
  • Flow measures loudness, tempo and energy on your PC, then follows the shape you pick: Smooth only, Build up, Waves, Peak or Steady
  • Every hand-over is graded smooth, noticeable or a jump. Drag files right on the chart, and save the orders you like
  • Lanes of stream tags sit under the chart: Role comes with tags like Opener, Centerpiece and Closer, and you can add your own, like a Mood lane, each tag with its color and key. A tag's Place (first, last, at a peak) tells Flow where it goes
  • Up next slips a file in without changing the playlist: pick a file and press U, or Shift+U to play it next

  • The set log writes down what played and when, so you can answer "what was that 35 minutes ago?" and copy it as a list or as chapters
  • Click a file to look at it without interrupting; double-click sends it to Stage

Share privately

Send files to a friend. Nobody else can open them.

Pick files or a whole collection. Vault packs them into an encrypted 7z archive on your PC, uploads it to gofile once you confirm, and gives you a ready message with the link and the password.

  • Before packing, Vault checks for personal details (location, device, names, folder paths) and removes them from the packed copies. Your originals are never changed
  • AES-256 with the file names hidden, tested before upload; the password never leaves your PC
  • Copy as block puts the link and the password together, in a format you can write yourself
  • My shares lists what you have uploaded, with copy, open, delete and an optional delete timer
  • Off until you turn it on, and nothing is uploaded unless you start a share and confirm it

Optional · Local AI

Want more? Add a local AI.

Vault works fully without it. Turn the AI scan on and a small vision model on your own PC watches every file and writes down what's in it - the scene, the mood, the action - so you search by meaning, not filenames. Set it Off, On, or Auto, which pauses while you watch.

  • Semantic search - "amateur pov" or "slow build" surfaces the right scenes, no tags required
  • Off means Vault never scans and never asks; Auto stops the moment something plays and carries on after
  • Runs on LM Studio, Ollama or vLLM, on your own GPU. Nothing is sent anywhere

The small stuff that adds up

And more. A lot more.

The smaller features that add up, each in a line.

Mini player

Pop a video into a floating corner window and keep browsing. Drag it and it snaps to the nearest corner, or Pop out.

Resume and view tracking

Picks up where you left off; counts real engagement, not thumbnail flips. Hot and Done mark the moments that nearly did you in.

Hover scrubbing

YouTube-style frame previews on every card - in the library, the game picker, and the studio.

Plays almost anything

AVI, TS, FLV and MKV play right in the app, with AC3 and DTS audio converted on the fly.

Toy sync

Lovense support rides the same beat grid as the beat bar - your hardware follows the music.

Import and export playlists

Turn a VLC or PotPlayer playlist into a collection, or export one to play where Vault isn't.

Folder watchers

Added, changed, moved and deleted files are picked up on their own. The eye in the header shows what changed.

Drag and drop import

Drop files or folders onto the window, or use the folder picker, and cards appear while the AI catches up. On Windows, hold still for a second to drop them exactly where they go.

Saved searches

One-click colored chips for the filter combos you use most.

Duplicate finder

Catches re-encodes and resizes that filename matching misses - confirmed copies share notes automatically.

Moved your files?

Quick move swaps one folder for another; Deep search finds renamed files. Your data follows, no rescan.

Bring your own AI

Works with LM Studio, Ollama, or vLLM - point it at one endpoint or several and it load-balances across GPUs.

Parallel scanning

Vault loads the vision model more than once when your GPU has room, so files scan side by side.

Retry what failed

Show only the files that failed or were never scanned, then rescan them in one click. The scan panel lists each file it couldn't read, with Retry.

Hand-fix anything

The AI got one wrong? Edit any field from the Info panel, or rescan a single file - no full re-run.

Privacy mode, your way

Choose exactly what it hides, from file paths and notes to thumbnails and file names.

Thumbnail settings

Make them for every file, only when shown, or not at all, and cap the space they take.

One portable app

A single executable. No installer, no services, no registry - your library is a folder you can copy, back up, or shred.

Windows and Linux

Runs on Windows, or on Linux from a tar.gz: extract it and run ./Vault.

Two Vaults at once

Give each library its own name and port. The name shows in the header and on the lock screen.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? anywhere for the full list. The keys for the screen you are on come first.

Activity button

One button in the top bar for everything running: AI scan, shares, face search, adding files and the trash queue.

Exit Vault

A button at the bottom left of Settings stops Vault cleanly. It asks first, and it's the way to stop Vault on Linux when there is no window to close.

Built for big libraries

Very large collections open without stalling.
